Build evidence

What we've actually proved

Last reviewed 18 November 2026

Physical fit observed

Stacked PCB spacers

Three 17.4 mm and three 14.2 mm spacers have been printed and assembled with the real boards. Visual fit appears good; mechanical retention and cable strain are not qualified.

Physical fit observed

SPS30 guide width

The 42.0 mm nominal guide opening was tested with the real SPS30. It slides comfortably. This proves width fit only, not airflow or retention.

CAD study only

PoE-inclusive packaging

A separate lower PoE bay is included in the latest layout audit. The three-board assembly measured 50.8 mm in depth without PoE. The combined posed component envelope is not a physical enclosure-fit result.

Not run

Our design principle

As small as it can be. As capable as it needs to be.

Accuracy, airflow, repairability and low manufacturing waste matter more than an artificially thin case. The full enclosure is not ready for printing; only specific test pieces are being validated.
